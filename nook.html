Nook isn’t just a better-looking calendar app. Nook frees up people's time, so high-performing teams can focus on what actually matters.

In a crowded market of productivity tools, we identified opportunities to differentiate our product by focusing on a user-centric approach, enhancing functionality, and creating a seamless experience that merges efficiency with delight. This led to pivotal updates like the personal booking page and an improved onboarding flow. These changes contributed directly to increased site traffic and monthly active users (MAU).

Our work was recognized when Nook Calendar earned the coveted titles of #1 Product of the Day and #3 Product of the Week on Product Hunt.

Onboarding & User Engagement

Streamlining Permissions and User Education

One of the key challenges during onboarding was creating a flow that not only guided users through authentication and privacy permissions but also communicated the value of the product in an engaging way. I redesigned the onboarding process to be more intuitive, balancing technical requirements with user motivation.

To showcase the core functionality of the "People Bar", I developed an animated sequence that demonstrated its collaborative features before asking users to invite or add colleagues. By visually highlighting this key feature upfront, we reduced user hesitation and improved engagement. Through this work, I created new internal standards within our design system for how Nook commuciates with new users.

Personal Booking Page

Wizard Setup and Booking Experience

The personal booking page was an opportunity to distinguish Nook Calendar in a market dominated by tools like Calendly. I led the design for a clean and intuitive setup wizard that allowed users to:

  1. Claim a personal URL
  2. Configure their booking page preferences with minimal effort

Recognizing the importance of a mobile-friendly experience, I adjusted the flow for Nook’s iOS app to ensure users had the same intuitive setup process regardless of device.

I designed steps to claim your URL and set up a personal booking page

Designing a Friendlier Booking Experience

We also addressed common criticisms around our competitor, Calendly’s somewhat impersonal booking process. I designed a welcome page to provide a warmer, more approachable first impression for visitors before they selected a time to meet. This subtle shift aligned Nook’s design language with its values: efficiency meets human connection.

Reflections
