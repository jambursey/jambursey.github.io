Hi - I'm James!

For the past 5 years, I've worked across iOS, Android, web and MacOS apps designing products that meet the needs of real people.

As a Design Lead at Pilot, I’m solving problems for travellers to create the ultimate collaborative travel app. Helping groups of travellers to discover, plan and book, all in one place has been a labour of love for over two years.

At my core, I believe in making the design process collaborative, iterative and fun. I’m passionate about crafting designs that inspire and empower, and I’m always excited to explore new challenges.
